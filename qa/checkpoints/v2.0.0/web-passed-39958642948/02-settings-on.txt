AI Gijinka · Shared World, Individual Personas
v1.7.0-alpha.1

dsh-kujira-persona

Switch AI characters with your selected DSH model, and keep developing them through conversation. WORLD: Shared names, community impressions, jokes and relationships help characters recognize one another. CHARACTERS: Each has their own personality, preferences and memories. Versions within the same model family share one character by default. EXPAND IT IN DSH CHAT: Ask the AI to add shared lore, change a persona, create your own character or assign a character to a custom model. No manual file editing is needed. For example: ‘Make the dragon girl more lively’, ‘Add our new agreement to the shared world’, or ‘Create Stella with this description and use her for the current model’. Presets are only a starting point. When your wishes differ, your choices come first.
